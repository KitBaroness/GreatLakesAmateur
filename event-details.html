GREAT LAKES AMATEUR Tournament Information

MONDAY, August 17 – WEDNESDAY, August 19

Entry Fee $299 (Includes tournament rounds, range balls, player gift and lunch daily)

Great Lakes Amateur

Dates: Monday, August 17 – Wednesday, August 19

Location: Eagle Crest Golf Course, Ypsilanti, MI

Format: 54-Hole Stroke Play, at Eagle Crest Golf Course. In the event of a tie for first place upon the completion of play, there will be a hole-by-hole sudden death playoff beginning on a hole(s) determined by the rules committee prior to the opening round.

Tournament Overview

Join us at the first Great Lakes Amateur, a premier 54-hole stroke play competition held over three days. The event will feature 18 holes per day. This tournament is open to any amateurs male or female, providing an active USGA Handicap Index of 6.0 or lower for male's 10.0 for females at the time of entry to be eligible.

Tournament Schedule

  • Monday, August 17 – Round 1 (18 Holes) 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM 1st tee
  • Tuesday, August 18 – Round 2 (18 Holes) 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM 1st tee
  • Wednesday, August 19 – Final Round (18 Holes) 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM 1st tee

Entry Requirements

  • Amateurs: Must have an active USGA/RandA Handicap Index of Male's 6.0 or lower Female 10.0 or lower at the time of entry.
  • Field Size: 90 Players—register early to secure your spot!

Course Details

Several holes border the banks of Ford Lake, offering beautiful views throughout your round. Those include the course's signature hole, the par-5 16th, which runs along the lake and has a peninsular green. The hole was recently cited by USA Today as one of Detroit's "Dream 18-Most Memorable Holes." Eagle Crest Golf Club is an excellent value, where you can enjoy golf, food, and drink in a grand setting that continues to receive Golf Digest Magazine's Four-Star "Best Places To Play" rating. Eagle Crest, owned and operated by Eastern Michigan University, is the home course of EMU's women's and men's golf teams. It features an 18-hole layout measuring 7186 yards 75.1 rating with distinctive holes, stunning views of Ford Lake, and an extensive practice facility consisting of a three-tier driving range, a short game area with a pitching/chipping green, and two practice putting greens. In a recent article, Free Press reporter Carlos Monarrez said, "what makes this (Eagle Crest) an outstanding course is the marriage between challenge and beauty that comes in the form of natural adornment."

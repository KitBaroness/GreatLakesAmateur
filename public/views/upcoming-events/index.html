Summer Golf Camps

GameAbove Performance Center at Eagle Crest Golf Course

Players will sharpen their skills, make new friends, and enjoy a week full of learning and laughs, from first swings to advanced play.

Led by a coaching staff with over 30 years of professional golf experience and 20+ years of collegiate coaching expertise, this camp combines top-level instruction with family-friendly activities that make learning the game enjoyable for everyone.

Camp Highlights

  • Hands-on instruction from seasoned golf professionals
  • Parent-child team challenges and fun competitions
  • Skill-building sessions focused on fundamentals, short game, and on-course strategy
  • Family-friendly environment fostering teamwork and lasting memories

Whether you are introducing your child to golf or refining your own game alongside them, this camp offers a perfect way to share quality time and grow your love for the sport together.

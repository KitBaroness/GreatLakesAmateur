Great Lakes Amateur

Dates: Monday, August 17 - Wednesday, August 19

Location: Eagle Crest Golf Course, Ypsilanti, MI

Format: 54-hole stroke play at Eagle Crest Golf Course. In the event of a tie for first place, there will be a hole-by-hole sudden death playoff beginning on a hole selected by the rules committee prior to the opening round.

Tournament Overview

Join us for the Great Lakes Amateur, a premier 54-hole stroke play competition held over three days. The event features 18 holes per day and is open to eligible amateur competitors with an active USGA Handicap Index of 6.0 or lower for men and 10.0 or lower for women at the time of entry.

Entry Requirements

Distance Measuring Devices

A player may obtain distance information by use of a distance-measuring device. If, during a stipulated round, a player uses a distance-measuring device to gauge or measure other conditions that might affect play, such as elevation change or wind speed, the player is in breach of Rule 14-3.

Course Details

Several holes border the banks of Ford Lake, offering beautiful views throughout your round. Those include the course's signature hole, the par-5 16th, which runs along the lake and has a peninsular green. The hole was recently cited by USA Today as one of Detroit's "Dream 18-Most Memorable Holes." Eagle Crest Golf Club is an excellent value, where you can enjoy golf, food, and drink in a grand setting that continues to receive Golf Digest Magazine's Four-Star "Best Places To Play" rating. Eagle Crest, owned and operated by Eastern Michigan University, is the home course of EMU's women's and men's golf teams. It features an 18-hole layout measuring 7186 yards 75.1 rating with distinctive holes, stunning views of Ford Lake, and an extensive practice facility consisting of a three-tier driving range, a short game area with a pitching/chipping green, and two practice putting greens. In a recent article, Free Press reporter Carlos Monarrez said, "what makes this (Eagle Crest) an outstanding course is the marriage between challenge and beauty that comes in the form of natural adornment."

Refunds will be provided for U.S. Amateur qualifiers and anyone who notifies us before August 1, 2026.

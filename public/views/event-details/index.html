About the event

Tournament Overview

Join us for the Great Lakes Amateur, a premier 54-hole stroke play competition held over three days. The event features 18 holes per day and is open to eligible amateur competitors with an active USGA Handicap Index of 6.0 or lower for men and 10.0 or lower for women at the time of entry.

In the event of a tie for first place, there will be a hole-by-hole sudden death playoff beginning on a hole selected by the rules committee prior to the opening round.

Before you tee off

Policies & On-Course Rules

Practice Rounds

Practice rounds are based on Eagle Crest Golf Course availability. Contact Eagle Crest at (734) 487-2441.

Sunday, August 16, 2026: $50

Caddies

A player may bring a caddie. Participants may carry their own bag or use a push/pull cart.

Distance Measuring Devices

A player may obtain distance information by use of a distance-measuring device. If, during a stipulated round, a player uses a device to gauge or measure other conditions that might affect play — such as elevation change or wind speed — the player is in breach of Rule 14-3.

Inclement Weather Release

Participants assume the risk of injury and casualty that may be caused by inclement weather and release the Great Lakes Amateur and its affiliates from liability related thereto.

Host venue

Course Details

Several holes border the banks of Ford Lake, offering beautiful views throughout your round — including the signature par-5 16th, which runs along the lake and has a peninsular green. The hole was recently cited by USA Today as one of Detroit's "Dream 18 — Most Memorable Holes."

Eagle Crest Golf Club continues to receive Golf Digest Magazine's Four-Star "Best Places To Play" rating. Owned and operated by Eastern Michigan University, it is the home course of EMU's women's and men's golf teams. The 18-hole layout measures 7,186 yards (75.1 rating) with distinctive holes, stunning views of Ford Lake, and an extensive practice facility with a three-tier driving range, short game area, and two practice putting greens.

Refunds will be provided for U.S. Amateur qualifiers and anyone who notifies us before August 1, 2026.
